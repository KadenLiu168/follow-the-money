# Digest Compression Contract

These rules apply once to all five Feed domains. Compression is editorial
presentation owned by the Host Agent over the prepared `DigestContext` version
`2`, its `content.updates`, and their current validated Feed evidence; it is not
a deterministic Feed result.

## Non-Exhaustive Selection

`content.updates` is the substantive input, and the Digest is a selective view
of it. The Digest is not required to display every prepared update, every Feed
item, or every domain. Do not rebuild a complete textual projection of the Feed
and do not reconcile per-domain totals, individual/consolidated/omitted counts,
or another representation-accounting formula. Full evidence accounting remains
in the authoritative Feed.

## Claim Support and Traceability

Every factual claim presented, whether summarized, consolidated, or compressed,
must remain supported by one or more prepared updates and traceable through them
to their supporting Feed item IDs and eligible source provenance. Keep
statement-local provenance or attribution close enough to the claim it supports.
Do not merge updates into a statement whose factual content they do not support.
Consolidation may remove repetition, but it may not create a new fact, causal
link, sentiment, direction, or market interpretation.

## Omission

A prepared update that is not presented, and a Feed item that preparation did
not expose as a current update, need not be accounted for visibly. Do not
describe an omission as unimportant, irrelevant, insignificant, invalid, or a
relevance or importance determination. Editorial grouping, ordering,
consolidation, and omission are presentation choices, not Feed results.

The same rules apply when `content.updates` is empty: report that no
deterministically eligible current update is available and disclose only the
compact status or limitation that applies. Do not use a historical Feed,
checkpoint, external source, or `raw_metadata` to fill a compressed or omitted
item.
