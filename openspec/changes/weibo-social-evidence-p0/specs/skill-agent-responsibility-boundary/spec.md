## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Skill owns only the validated Evidence Feed
The Skill SHALL own the accepted deterministic semantics and trust guarantees of Feed production and current published-Feed consumption, including typed, versioned, non-persisted v3 preparation wholly derived from one validated Feed. Preparation SHALL own only contract-defined current-membership classification, reader-facing unit construction, reference-state classification, compact domain status, material limitations, and canonical ordering. These structural operations SHALL remain bounded Feed consumption machinery and SHALL NOT become a second evidence capability, semantic inference layer, editorial engine, financial-analysis capability, or Agent runtime.

#### Scenario: Skill responsibility is traced
- **WHEN** repository behavior and Host-Agent integration are inspected
- **THEN** the Skill owns Feed evidence and deterministic eligibility structuring but no prose, importance, ranking, financial interpretation, or orchestration

### Requirement: Host Agent owns evidence-preserving digest presentation
The Host Agent SHALL receive one validated-Feed-bound `DigestContext` version `3`, use `content.updates` as its default substantive input, and own semantic-support assessment, concise summarization, source- or explicit-document-type presentation grouping, headings, readability ordering, source-supported consolidation, formatting, and user-facing Digest presentation. It SHALL use `status.domains` and `status.limitations` only for applicable reader-facing disclosure. A current Social acquisition-failure limitation SHALL always be disclosed with its affected account selection, exact window, sanitized reason and no-automatic-backfill policy, including when updates are empty; editorial compression SHALL NOT omit it. It SHALL NOT own current-window membership classification, reference-state classification, Feed-wide omission accounting, or exhaustive compression reconciliation and SHALL NOT introduce importance, significance, anomaly, causality, market impact, prediction, investment recommendation, trading judgment, or another unsupported authority.

#### Scenario: Host Agent produces the digest
- **WHEN** one v3 context is summarized for a user
- **THEN** presentation remains evidence-preserving and reader-first without reclassifying Feed evidence or printing mandatory exhaustive accounting

### Requirement: Feed authority remains bounded after consumption
Deterministic v3 structuring SHALL preserve exact Feed identity and shall derive every exposed unit, source, status, limitation, and trace only from the validated Feed without upgrading source authority. Every presented context evidence value SHALL remain traceable to one or more supporting Feed item IDs and eligible original-source provenance. Feed evidence that is not exposed as a current unit SHALL remain authoritative in the Feed and SHALL not be characterized by its omission. Any Host-Agent modification, synthesis, or derivation SHALL remain Host-Agent-owned and SHALL NOT be represented as unchanged Feed or `DigestContext` output.

#### Scenario: Feed evidence is transformed
- **WHEN** validated evidence is classified into current units or compact non-current status and the Host Agent summarizes it
- **THEN** neither preparation nor presentation adds evidence authority, and omitted Feed evidence retains its original Feed authority without a presentation judgment
